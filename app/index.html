Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough, and there is no mission replay — so keep a save at the start and end of each chapter (PowerPyx recommends 10 saves: 2 per chapter) and do the Chapter 1 meditation below before Chapter 1 ends. Everything else can be cleaned up in free roam after the credits. Difficulty is 4/10, about 200 hours in all: 60–80 hours of story, about 100 hours of 100% checklist, about 10 hours of cleanup.
